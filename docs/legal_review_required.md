# Legal review required before public launch

Status: product-accurate v2 policy drafts are implemented locally. They are not
counsel-approved and must not be described as universally compliant.

Internal checklist only. Do not copy unresolved positions into store listings.

- Global launch jurisdiction analysis; governing law, forum, disputes,
  warranties, liability and statutory rights.
- Lawful bases, consent/acknowledgement wording and international transfers.
- Operator/controller presentation and whether a physical/legal contact,
  privacy representative or other contact is required.
- Binding retention periods, legal holds, deletion exceptions and consent receipts.
- Attendance and Reliability correction/contest rights and support commitments.
- Minors/18+ handling when an age assertion is false or minor data is received.
- Service-provider roles, Firebase/Google/Apple disclosures and international transfers.
- Crashlytics provider-side collection and FCM/APNs processing.
- Private-location obligations, sports/physical-activity liability and
  third-party venue liability.
- Material-change/re-acceptance requirements and store-specific privacy disclosures.
- Precise-location classification for match venues and selected place coordinates.
- Production processor configuration and data-processing agreements.
- The Mexican Spanish Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Community Guidelines,
  account-deletion page, acknowledgement wording, retention wording,
  moderation wording, and emergency disclaimer require human/legal review
  before public distribution. The translations intentionally preserve the same
  v2 versions, effective date, operator, and contact as English.
