# Legal review required before public launch

Internal checklist only. Do not copy unresolved positions into store listings.

- Governing law, jurisdiction, disputes, warranties, liability and statutory rights.
- Lawful bases, consent/acknowledgement wording and international transfers.
- Whether an operator physical/legal contact is required.
- Binding retention periods, legal holds, deletion exceptions and consent receipts.
- Closed-beta participant terms and store-specific privacy disclosures.
- Precise-location classification for match venues and selected place coordinates.
- Production processor configuration and data-processing agreements.
- The Mexican Spanish Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Community Guidelines,
  account-deletion page, acknowledgement wording, retention wording,
  moderation wording, and emergency disclaimer require human/legal review
  before beta distribution. The translations intentionally preserve the same
  beta versions, effective date, operator, and contact as English.
# Attendance V2 review

Counsel/privacy review is required before public launch for the collection of private participant attendance claims, server-adjudicated attended/no-show outcomes, their use in the public summarized Reliability percentage, dispute/correction handling, deletion behavior, and retention duration. Raw evidence is private and is not shown in profiles, notifications, logs, or Match Details. This is a factual product description, not a compliance conclusion. Legal document versions are unchanged by this engineering phase.
