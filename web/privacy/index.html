PadelX Privacy Policy

Closed beta · privacy-beta-v1 · Effective September 14, 2026

PadelX is operated for this beta by Paul Desautels. Contact support.padelx@gmail.com.

Who may use PadelX

PadelX is intended only for people aged 18 or older. Registration requires an affirmative 18+ confirmation. We do not collect date of birth, independently verify age, or provide parental consent.

Data we process

We process account email and UID; profile, avatar, level and preferences; city and optional area labels, Google Places identifiers and selected coordinates; device location when you invoke nearby features; match venues, matches, requests, friendships, blocks and shared history; plaintext Direct Messages and Match Chat; notifications; ratings and aggregates; push-device identifiers when configured; and safety reports, evidence, review and enforcement records.

Visibility and discovery

Profile and match information is visible to authenticated PadelX users as needed for the service. Turning discovery off removes your profile from Find Players. Players may still see it through matches, friendships, messages, invitations, or shared history. Individual rating submissions are private; rating average and count may be displayed.

Location and providers

Search text, country, session information and optional coordinate bias may be sent to Google Places. Match venue information and coordinates are shown to authenticated users. Current device location is used when you request nearby features. Firebase and Google infrastructure, Apple services, and device location services process data to operate, secure and deliver PadelX.

Purposes

We use data for authentication, profiles, discovery, matches, communication, notifications, ratings, support, safety, abuse prevention, moderation, enforcement, security and account deletion.

Messages, reports and moderation

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized systems and reviewers may process content submitted in a safety report. Reports may result in account restrictions, but report count alone does not automatically create enforcement.

Retention and deletion

Profiles generally remain for the account life. Messages remain for the service or conversation lifecycle and are neutralized as described on the deletion page. Notifications currently remain until cleanup or account deletion; a 90-day cleanup target is not yet automated. Unresolved reports remain until resolution. Resolved evidence has a 12-month target and minimal moderation audit data a 24-month target; these targets are not yet automatically enforced. Provider logs and backups expire under provider lifecycles.

Deletion removes or neutralizes active account data, but anonymized match history, message tombstones, safety evidence, moderation/audit, fraud, security, legal records, provider logs and backups may remain where necessary.

Requests and security

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, email support.padelx@gmail.com. Never email your password. We use provider transport protections, Firebase Authentication, access rules, App Check and server-controlled operations. No system can guarantee absolute security, and data may be processed internationally where service providers operate.

Changes

We may update this beta policy. Material changes will be presented with an updated version or effective date.
