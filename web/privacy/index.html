PadelX Privacy Policy

Beta · privacy-beta-v2 · Effective September 18, 2026

PadelX is operated for this beta by Paul Desautels. Contact support.padelx@gmail.com.

Who may use PadelX

PadelX is intended only for people aged 18 or older. Registration requires an affirmative 18+ confirmation. We do not collect date of birth, independently verify age, or provide parental consent.

Data we process

We process account email, UID and authentication state; profile, avatar, level, preferred side, play preferences and bio; city and optional area labels, Places identifiers, selected coordinates, travel radius and device location when you invoke nearby features; Quick Match availability, mode, partner consent, invitations, offers, confirmations and compatibility state; match schedules, participants, teams and venues; plaintext Direct Messages and Match Chat; friendships, blocks, invitations and shared history; player level, subjective ratings and aggregates; objective Reliability events and summarized projections; private Attendance submissions, evidence and resolutions; notification preferences, device and notification identifiers and delivery-processing records; safety reports, evidence, review, enforcement and audit records; diagnostic information in enabled release builds; legal acceptance; and account-deletion workflow records.

Visibility and discovery

Profile and match information is visible to authenticated PadelX users as needed for the service. Turning discovery off removes your profile from Find Players, but players may still see it through matches, friendships, messages, invitations or shared history. Individual subjective rating submissions are private; rating average and count may be displayed. Reliability may be displayed as New player or as a summarized status or rounded percentage. Underlying Reliability events and raw Attendance evidence are restricted.

Quick Match and recommendations

We use availability, approximate location and travel constraints, player level, party or partner information, preferred side, schedule conflicts, Reliability and other relevant compatibility information to identify and prioritize compatible Quick Match candidates and assign teams. Reliability summarizes objective or corroborated commitment activity; it is not a skill rating, subjective player rating or automatic enforcement decision. We do not publish proprietary matching weights and do not guarantee a match or perfect team balance.

Reliability and Attendance

Reliability events may reflect confirmed commitments, completed Attendance, cancellation timing, successful replacement and established no-show outcomes. A summarized Reliability result may be visible to verified users and may influence matchmaking order; the underlying event history is restricted. PadelX may update the methodology over time, subject to applicable notice or consent requirements.

Eligible final-roster participants may submit whether a match occurred and who played. These submissions, server resolution and any resulting Reliability outcome are used for service integrity, Reliability and review of demonstrable errors or established abuse where appropriate. Raw Attendance evidence is restricted but is not promised to be anonymous. A single negative claim is not automatically conclusive, conflicting or insufficient evidence can remain unresolved, and app inactivity alone does not establish a no-show. Attendance does not automatically create a safety report or enforcement action.

Location and Places

We use approximate city or area, selected coordinates and travel radius for discovery and matching. Current device location is used only when you request a nearby feature; PadelX does not claim to track location continuously or in the background. Public venue information may appear on a match. Exact custom private venue information, including an address or coordinates, is restricted to authorized participants as needed for a confirmed match.

Search text, country, session information and optional coordinate bias or restriction may be sent to Google Places to return and validate location or venue candidates. Google and device/platform services may process related request and technical data under their services and policies.

Push notifications

When push is configured and enabled, we process a device or notification identifier, platform and application identity, locale, preferences, timestamps and delivery-processing records to register devices, send notifications, manage preferences, troubleshoot delivery and remove stale or invalid registrations. You may disable push in PadelX or device settings. Delivery depends on permissions, network and provider availability and is not guaranteed; the current in-app and server state remains authoritative for expiring offers.

Crash and error diagnostics

In enabled native release builds, PadelX may send crash and error diagnostics through Crashlytics. PadelX intentionally attaches limited operational context such as environment, build or version, and sanitized error context. Infrastructure providers may process additional technical or device diagnostic information under their services and policies. PadelX does not currently add a separate analytics SDK.

Service providers

PadelX uses Firebase and Google infrastructure, including Authentication, Firestore, Functions, Storage, App Check, Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics; Google Places; device location and platform services; and Apple services where applicable. These services process data to authenticate users, store and secure data, run server operations, host files, validate application traffic, deliver notifications, diagnose errors and provide location results. Provider roles, international processing and retention remain subject to their services, policies and final legal review.

Messages, reports, blocking and moderation

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized systems and reviewers may process content included in a safety report. A report is not proof and report count alone does not automatically create enforcement. Attendance evidence, safety reports, Reliability and disciplinary enforcement remain separate systems. Blocking limits future ordinary discovery and contact but does not erase legitimate historical matches, messages, ratings, Attendance or Reliability records.

Purposes

We use data for authentication, eligibility and legal acceptance; profiles; discovery and matchmaking; matches and teams; communication; notifications; ratings and Reliability; Attendance; support; safety; abuse and fraud prevention; moderation and enforcement; security and diagnostics; and staged account deletion.

Retention and deletion

Retention varies by data category and purpose. We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, safety, dispute and error review, security, fraud prevention, deletion processing, audit and legal obligations, subject to applicable requirements. Profiles generally remain for the account life. Messages remain for the service or conversation lifecycle and are neutralized as described on the deletion page. Notifications remain until cleanup or account deletion; a 90-day cleanup target is not yet automated. Unresolved reports remain until resolution. Resolved evidence has a 12-month target and minimal moderation audit data a 24-month target; these targets are not yet automatically enforced. Exact retention periods for other categories remain under legal/privacy review. Provider logs and backups expire under provider lifecycles.

Deletion begins through the product deletion process and is staged. Access may be disabled before backend cleanup completes. Active account data is removed or neutralized through that process. Limited historical, safety, audit, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, legal, provider-log or backup information may remain where reasonably necessary and legally permitted. No instantaneous completion deadline is promised.

Requests and security

For access, correction, deletion, Attendance or Reliability concerns, or privacy questions, email support.padelx@gmail.com. PadelX may review and correct demonstrable technical or data errors, or established abuse, where appropriate, but does not guarantee a correction or response time. Never email your password. We use provider transport protections, Firebase Authentication, access rules, App Check and server-controlled operations. No system can guarantee absolute security, and data may be processed internationally where service providers operate.

Changes

We may update this beta policy and the Reliability methodology. Material changes will be presented with an updated version or effective date and any notice or acceptance required by applicable law.
