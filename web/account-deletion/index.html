Delete your PadelX account

Closed beta · Updated September 14, 2026

Delete in the app

Open Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app

Email support.padelx@gmail.com from your account email. We will verify control of the account without asking for your password or identity documents by default. Never email your password.

What happens

Deletion removes or neutralizes your account/profile, avatar, social relationships, future match participation, ratings involving the account, push registration and normal account access.

PadelX may retain anonymized historical match information, anonymized or tombstoned messages needed for remaining participants, safety reports and evidence, moderation/audit, fraud, security or legal records, and provider logs/backups until normal expiration. Deletion is a staged process and is not an immediate universal erasure.

Use the same support address for access, correction, deletion and privacy questions.
