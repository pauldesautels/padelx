Community Guidelines

community-beta-v2 · Effective September 18, 2026

PadelX is operated by Paul Desautels. Contact support.padelx@gmail.com.

PadelX is for people 18 and older. Treat players respectfully.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, intimidation or targeted abuse.
  • Hate, discrimination, unwanted sexual content or sexual harassment.
  • Threats, violence or deliberately unsafe conduct.
  • Spam, scams, deception, fraud or impersonation.
  • Sharing another person’s private information or unnecessarily redistributing exact private or residential venue information.
  • Misleading profiles, matches, locations, times, costs, levels or availability.
  • Harassment, threats, spam or scams in Direct Messages or Match Chat.
  • Retaliation for blocking or reporting, or malicious or fabricated reports.
  • Knowingly false or coordinated Attendance submissions intended to manipulate another player’s Reliability. A good-faith disagreement, mistake or conflicting recollection is not automatically misconduct.

Reliability, Attendance and ratings

Player level describes playing ability. Subjective star ratings are player feedback. Reliability is a separate commitment signal based on objective or corroborated PadelX activity, such as confirmed commitments, cancellation timing, successful replacement and resolved Attendance outcomes. Reliability may influence matchmaking order, but low Reliability is not itself misconduct and does not automatically suspend or ban an account.

Submit Attendance information honestly. Attendance concerns whether a match occurred and who played; it is separate from a safety report. Conflicting or insufficient evidence may remain unresolved and does not automatically establish misconduct.

Private venues and real-world safety

Only provide a private or residential location when you are authorized to use and share it for the match. Do not misuse or unnecessarily redistribute exact private venue information. Exercise reasonable judgment when meeting people. PadelX does not verify every person, host, location or safety condition.

Blocking, reports and enforcement

Respect another player’s decision to block or stop communicating. Blocking limits future ordinary discovery and contact, but does not erase legitimate historical matches, messages, ratings, Attendance or Reliability records. A report is not proof, report volume alone does not automatically create enforcement, and Attendance or Reliability does not automatically create a disciplinary sanction. PadelX may review reported conduct and restrict access when appropriate.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. PadelX reporting and support are not emergency services.

Questions, safety concerns, or Attendance and Reliability concerns: support.padelx@gmail.com. PadelX may review demonstrable errors or established abuse where appropriate, but does not guarantee a correction or response time.
