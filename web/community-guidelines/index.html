Community Guidelines

community-beta-v1 · Effective September 14, 2026

PadelX is for adults 18 and older. Treat players respectfully.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, intimidation or targeted abuse.
  • Hate, discrimination, unwanted sexual content or sexual harassment.
  • Threats, violence or deliberately unsafe conduct.
  • Spam, scams, deception, fraud or impersonation.
  • Sharing another person’s private information or exposing private residential locations.
  • Misleading profiles, matches, locations, times, costs, levels or availability.
  • Harassment, threats, spam or scams in Direct Messages or Match Chat.
  • Retaliation for blocking/reporting or malicious/fabricated reports.

Safety and enforcement

Use reasonable judgment when meeting people. PadelX may review reported conduct and apply a temporary suspension or persistent access restriction. Future participation behavior may later inform product improvements; no Reliability feature currently exists.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. PadelX reporting and support are not emergency services.

Questions, safety concerns and appeals: support.padelx@gmail.com.
