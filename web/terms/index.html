Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 and truthfully confirm that requirement. Keep your account secure and provide accurate profile information.

Beta service

PadelX is a beta service intended for users globally. Features may change, be interrupted, or be withdrawn. PadelX currently supports profiles, player and match discovery, organizer-created matches, Quick Match, messages, social connections, ratings, Reliability, Attendance confirmations, notifications, reports, moderation and deletion. PadelX does not currently process payments.

Quick Match, teams and commitments

Quick Match may automatically identify and assemble compatible players using availability, location and travel constraints, player level, party or partner requirements, Reliability and other relevant compatibility information. PadelX may assign teams, but does not guarantee that a match will be found, teams will be perfectly balanced, a venue will remain available, or a player will attend or behave appropriately. Find Matches remains a manual browsing path, and organizers may separately create matches.

Accepting or confirming a spot creates a PadelX commitment. Declining before acceptance or allowing an unaccepted offer to expire does not create the same confirmed commitment. After confirmation, cancellation timing may affect Reliability. PadelX may attempt to find a compatible replacement through AutoFill, and a successful replacement may affect how a cancellation is treated.

Reliability and Attendance

Player level describes playing ability. Reliability is a separate service signal based on objective or corroborated PadelX activity that may include confirmed commitments, completed Attendance, cancellation timing, successful replacement and established no-show outcomes. Reliability may influence matchmaking order. It is not a guarantee, skill rating, subjective popularity score or automatic disciplinary sanction. PadelX may update its methodology as the system improves, subject to any applicable notice or consent requirements.

After eligible matches, participants may be asked whether the match occurred and who played. PadelX may use corroborated Attendance information to resolve objective commitment outcomes. A single negative claim is not automatically conclusive; conflicting or insufficient evidence may remain unresolved, and app inactivity alone does not establish a no-show. Resolved outcomes may affect Reliability but remain separate from safety enforcement.

You may contact support.padelx@gmail.com about an Attendance or Reliability concern. PadelX may review relevant information and correct demonstrable technical or data errors, or established abuse, where appropriate. A correction or response time is not guaranteed.

Matches, venues and real-world safety

Provide accurate time, venue, level and availability information. Public venues and private, residential or free courts may be used. A person who provides a private location must be authorized to use and share it for the match. Exact private venue information is disclosed only as needed to authorized participants. Do not misuse or unnecessarily redistribute it. Participation arrangements are between users; PadelX does not verify every person, venue or safety condition. Exercise judgment when meeting others. PadelX is not an emergency service; contact local emergency services for immediate danger.

Your content

You are responsible for profile information, avatars, match content, messages, ratings, reports, Attendance submissions, private venue descriptions and other content you submit. You permit PadelX to host and process that content to operate and protect the service. Private Attendance and safety evidence is restricted and is not presented as public profile content.

Conduct, ratings, blocking and safety

Follow the Community Guidelines. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, scam, impersonate, expose private information, manipulate ratings or Attendance, retaliate, or submit malicious reports. Player level, Reliability and subjective player ratings are separate. Blocking limits future ordinary discovery and contact but does not erase legitimate historical matches, messages, ratings, Attendance or Reliability records. Blocking and reporting tools do not replace emergency services.

Offers and notifications

Quick Match offers may expire quickly. Notification delivery depends on device permissions and settings, network conditions and provider availability. A missing or delayed notification does not necessarily extend an offer; the current in-app and server state determines whether it remains available.

Moderation

PadelX may review reports and restrict, suspend or persistently restrict access when appropriate. A report is not proof, report volume alone does not automatically cause enforcement, and Reliability or Attendance does not automatically create a disciplinary sanction. Users may contact support about a concern, but no formal in-app appeal, guaranteed correction or response deadline is currently promised.
